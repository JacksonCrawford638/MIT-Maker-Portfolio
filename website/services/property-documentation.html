The work

Large parcels are hard to convey from eye level. We fly the property in a grid and process the imagery into precise aerial scans — a to-scale map of the whole footprint, a color model of the terrain, and a read on the ground cover. Invaluable for owners, land brokers, and recreational buyers.

What you get
  • 01 Orthomosaic mapOne to-scale, top-down image of the entire parcel, stitched from hundreds of overlapping aerial frames.
  • 02 Elevation & terrain modelA color heightmap revealing slope, drainage, and the high and low ground across the property.
  • 03 Vegetation analysisA plant-health scan that maps tree lines, crops, and ground cover so you can see what's thriving and what's bare.
