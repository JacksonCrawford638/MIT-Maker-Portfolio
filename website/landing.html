How it works

Quote to delivery.

A small studio means you talk to the person flying the drone and cutting the edit — start to finish.

STEP 01

Quote & book

Tell us the location, the goal, and roughly when. You get a clear estimate within one business day — approve it with a small deposit and we lock in your date.

STEP 02

Shoot

We show up and fly the site — usually a single half-day on location. If the weather won't cooperate, we reschedule, no fuss.

STEP 03

Edit & review

Color, cut, and sound. You preview the first edit in your portal and get a round of revisions to sign off on.

STEP 04

Deliver

Once it's approved and the balance is settled, full-resolution video, social cuts, and stills unlock in your portal — ready to download and post.
