Privacy Policy

Effective: June 27, 2026 · Last updated: June 27, 2026

SPAND Studio ("we," "us," "our") is a small drone media studio based in Washtenaw County, Michigan. This policy explains what information we collect when you use our website (spandstudio.com) and Client Portal, how we use it, and the choices you have. We've tried to write this in plain English. If anything is unclear, email us at hello@spandstudio.com.

The short version: We collect what we need to run your project — your account info, the messages and files you send us, and basic technical data to keep the portal working. We don't sell your information. We use Google's Firebase platform to host your account and project data. You can delete your account at any time.

1. Who this policy applies to

This policy applies to anyone who visits our website, uses our Client Portal, fills out our contact form, or otherwise shares information with us in connection with our services. If you sign up for an account on behalf of a business, this policy applies to you as the individual user.

Information we do not collect

We do not knowingly collect Social Security numbers, government IDs, payment card numbers (payments are handled by separate invoicing software outside the portal), precise GPS location, biometric data, or information from children under 16.

3. How we use your information

  • Run your account and project. Authenticate you, store your quote and project data, deliver finished files, and let us communicate with you about the work.
  • Customer support. Respond to your messages and troubleshoot problems.
  • Security and abuse prevention. Detect and prevent unauthorized access, fraud, and bot activity. Authentication metadata and IP addresses are used for this.
  • Improve our services. Understand how the portal is used so we can fix what's broken and add what's missing.
  • Legal compliance. Comply with applicable laws, respond to lawful requests from authorities, and enforce our Terms.

We do not use your information for behavioral advertising, and we do not build profiles of you for sale to third parties.

4. How we share your information

We share information only with:

  • Service providers who run our infrastructure. Specifically:
    • Google LLC (Firebase Authentication, Cloud Firestore, Cloud Storage, and related services) — hosts your account, stores your project data and files, and processes authentication. See Firebase's Privacy and Security page and Google's Privacy Policy.
    • Domain and email providers for sending verification, password reset, and project emails.
  • Legal requirements. If we receive a valid subpoena, court order, or similar legal process, or if disclosure is necessary to protect rights, safety, or property.
  • Business transfers. If we ever merge with, are acquired by, or sell assets to another company, your information may be transferred as part of that transaction. You'll be notified before any new entity gets your data.

We do not sell or rent your personal information, and we have not done so in the past twelve months.

5. How long we keep your information

We keep your information for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide the services. After your project ends:

  • Account information: retained until you delete your account.
  • Project files and deliverables: retained for 12 months after final delivery so you can re-download, then archived for an additional 12 months and then deleted, unless we've separately agreed to longer retention in writing.
  • Lead inquiries (people who message us without creating an account): retained for 24 months, then deleted.
  • Backups and logs: may persist for up to 90 days after deletion as part of routine system backups.

6. Your rights and choices

You can:

  • See what we have. Request a copy of the personal information we hold about you.
  • Correct it. Update your name, email, or any other information in your account settings, or ask us to fix something.
  • Delete it. Delete your account, which removes your profile and project data (subject to the retention timelines above and any legal obligations to retain records).
  • Opt out of marketing. We don't send marketing emails today, but if we ever do, every email will have an unsubscribe link.

To exercise any of these rights, email hello@spandstudio.com from the address on your account. We respond within 30 days.

If you live in California

California residents have additional rights under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA), as amended by the CPRA, including:

  • The right to know what categories and specific pieces of personal information we have collected about you, the sources, the business purpose, and the categories of third parties with whom we share it.
  • The right to delete personal information we have collected from you.
  • The right to correct inaccurate personal information.
  • The right to opt out of the sale or sharing of your personal information (we do not sell or share your information for cross-context behavioral advertising).
  • The right to non-discrimination for exercising any of these rights.

You may exercise these rights by emailing hello@spandstudio.com. We may need to verify your identity before fulfilling a request.

7. Children's privacy

Our services are intended for businesses and adults. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under the age of 16. If you believe we have collected information from a child, please contact us and we will delete it.

8. How we protect your information

We use industry-standard safeguards, including encryption in transit (HTTPS), encryption at rest (provided by Google Cloud), one-way password hashing, multi-factor sign-in via Google, email verification before portal access, and access controls that limit who in the studio can view your project data. No system is 100% secure, but we take this seriously and respond promptly to any incidents.

9. International users

Our services are operated from the United States and our data is stored on servers located in the United States. If you access the services from outside the U.S., you understand that your information will be transferred to and processed in the U.S. We don't currently market or actively offer our services to people outside the U.S.

10. Third-party links

Our site may link to third-party websites or services (for example, Google Sign-In). We're not responsible for the privacy practices of those sites. Read their policies before sharing information with them.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. When we do, we'll change the "Last updated" date at the top. If the changes are significant, we'll notify you by email or through a notice in the portal before the changes take effect. Continued use of the services after an update means you accept the updated policy.
